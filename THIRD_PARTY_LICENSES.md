### Universal Robots meshes

The UR5e visual and collision meshes come from `ur_description` and are covered by Universal
Robots A/S' *Terms and Conditions for Use of Graphical Documentation*
(<https://www.universal-robots.com/legal/terms-and-conditions/terms_and_conditions_for_use_of_graphical_documentation.txt>;
a copy ships in the image under `/opt/ros/jazzy/share/ur_description/meshes/*/LICENSE.txt`).
They allow use for simulation and visualisation and non-commercial academic research, forbid
using them to build competing products, and require the T&Cs to travel with any public
download. This repository does not copy the meshes; the image installs them from the ROS
package. The static results page draws the arm as a stick figure from forward kinematics and
contains no mesh.
