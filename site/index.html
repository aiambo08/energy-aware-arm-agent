Does energy feedback make an LLM robot agent use fewer watt-hours?

armbench, pre-registered evaluation f9-v1. Simulated UR5e (ROS 2 Jazzy + Gazebo Harmonic), 5 agents × 4 tasks × 20 locked seeds. LLM: Qwen/Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507, temperature 0. Source: github.com/aiambo08/energy-aware-arm-agent.

A: scripted baseline. B: LLM writes a program over 5 primitives. B+S: B with a frozen library of validated skills. C: B told baseline A's Wh for the task and the energy levers. C+S: C with skills. Energy is integrated from Gazebo joint effort (model A, η = 0.70) plus a constant P0 = 100 W; about 87 % of each episode's energy is P0·t, so time dominates.

Hypotheses (decided by the protocol, not by this page)

Energy: median relative paired difference (treatment vs control) and its 95 % bootstrap CI, worst case over the 6 sensitivity rows (model A/B × η 0.6/0.7/0.8). A saving is claimed only if every row's CI lies below 0 with success non-inferior (margin 0.10).
